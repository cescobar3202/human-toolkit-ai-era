title: 6. Build Trust
order: 60
toc: false
---

People still want to work with people they trust.

Trust comes from what you do repeatedly:

- Keep commitments
- Be clear about what you know
- Admit what you do not know
- Communicate early when something changes
- Make decisions people can understand

AI can help you communicate faster.

It cannot build your reputation for you.

Trust remains deeply human.
