---
title: 8. Keep the Human in the Loop
order: 80
toc: false
---

Use AI to extend your abilities, not to outsource your responsibility.

AI can research, draft, analyze, recommend, and automate.

But there are moments where a person still needs to:

- Verify the result
- Understand the consequences
- Make the decision
- Take responsibility
- Apply empathy and context

The goal is not to do everything manually.

The goal is to know where human involvement still matters.
