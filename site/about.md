# About the Talk

## The Human Toolkit for the AI Era

AI is getting better at writing, coding, analyzing information, generating ideas, and automating routine work.

That changes what people need to bring to the table.

This talk focuses on eight practical human skills that become more valuable as AI gets smarter:

- Clear thinking
- Better questions
- Communication
- Judgment
- Curiosity
- Trust
- Attention
- Human responsibility

The goal is practical: help people use AI without becoming passive users of it.

## Who Is This For?

This talk is designed for developers, managers, technical leaders, students, and professionals who want to work effectively with AI while strengthening the skills that remain distinctly human.
