---
title: 3. Communicate With Clarity
order: 30
toc: false
---

As AI produces more content, clear communication becomes more valuable.

More words do not automatically create more understanding.

Good communication starts by deciding:

- Who is this for?
- What do they need to understand?
- What action should they take?
- What can be removed?

AI can help draft, rewrite, summarize, and translate.

But you still need to decide what matters.

Clarity is a human responsibility.
