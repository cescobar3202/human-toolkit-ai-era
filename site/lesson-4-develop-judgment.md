AI can generate options. You still need to decide what makes sense.

That requires judgment.

Judgment means being able to evaluate:

- Whether information is reliable
- Whether an answer fits the context
- Whether the trade-offs are acceptable
- Whether something should be done at all

AI can help you compare choices, but it does not remove responsibility for the decision.

Use AI to widen your options.

Use judgment to choose between them.
