The quality of your questions often matters more than the sophistication of the tool.

AI responds to the direction you give it. If the question is vague, the answer usually will be too.

Better questions help you:

- Define the real problem
- Surface assumptions
- Explore alternatives
- Challenge weak reasoning
- Get more useful answers

Instead of asking:

> What should I do?

Try asking:

> What are the main options, what are the trade-offs, and what information would change the decision?

Good questions improve both human thinking and AI output.
