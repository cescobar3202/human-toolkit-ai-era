---
title: 5. Stay Curious
order: 50
toc: false
---

The tools will keep changing.

The ability to learn, experiment, and adapt matters more than memorizing one platform.

Curiosity helps you ask:

- Why does this work?
- What happens if I change this?
- Is there a better way?
- What am I missing?
- What can I learn from someone outside my field?

Do not build your value around knowing one tool.

Build it around your ability to keep learning.
