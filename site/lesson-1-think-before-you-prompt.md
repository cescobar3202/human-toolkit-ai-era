title: 1. Think Before You Prompt
order: 10
toc: false
---

Good AI output starts with clear human thinking.

Before asking AI to solve something, define what you are actually trying to achieve.

A better prompt often starts before the prompt itself.

Ask yourself:

- What problem am I trying to solve?
- What outcome do I want?
- What constraints matter?
- What information does the AI need?
- What would a good answer look like?

AI can accelerate your thinking, but it should not replace the thinking that gives the task direction.
