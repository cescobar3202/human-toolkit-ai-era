---
title: 7. Protect Your Attention
order: 70
toc: false
---

AI can accelerate work, but it can also create more noise.

More summaries, notifications, ideas, drafts, and possibilities compete for the same limited attention.

Protecting your attention means deciding:

- What deserves focus
- What can wait
- What should be automated
- What should be ignored
- When to disconnect from the tools

Speed is useful only when it helps you move in the right direction.

Your attention is still one of your most limited resources.
